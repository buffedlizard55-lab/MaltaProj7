Method & Sources

Rules followed, line by line

  1. San Francisco only. An event is included only if its official source places it inside the City & County of San Francisco. Regional events with non-SF venues were excluded and logged on the Flagged page.
  2. Two types only. Recurring = repeats weekly, monthly, or as a seasonal series within the year. Annual / Special = once-a-year festival, parade, race, or special edition. Seasonal series that are also annual traditions (e.g. Stern Grove) are classed Recurring because they repeat weekly within their season — the season is stated in the row.
  3. Official sources only. Priority: (1) City & County of San Francisco (sf.gov, sfrecpark.org, sfenvironment.org, sfpl.org); (2) the event’s own organiser/venue (e.g. Foodwise, SF Pride, SFFILM, Exploratorium, California Academy of Sciences); (3) SF Travel, San Francisco’s official destination organisation, for round-ups where the organiser page was not the source checked. Every table row links to the exact page used.
  4. No inference. If a source did not state cost, exact venue, or hours, the field is blank in data/events.json / data/events.csv and rendered as “unverified” on the site. See Flagged page.
  5. Dates. 2026 dates are shown only as stated by the linked source at check date (18 Oct 2026). Where two official sources disagree, the discrepancy is flagged, not silently resolved.

Analysis (only what the verified data supports)

  • The verified list is heaviest in Music and Food & Markets / Community recurring programming, and in summer–autumn annual festivals (June–October), reflecting the official sources checked — this is a property of those sources, not a claim about all SF activity.
  • Recurring anchors are weekly farmers markets, Thursday museum late-nights (Exploratorium / Cal Academy), library & City Guides programming, and seasonal free concert series (Stern Grove, Yerba Buena Gardens Festival, Sunday Streets).
  • Several flagship annual events are free to attend per their official sources (Pride, Stern Grove, Yerba Buena Gardens Festival, Sunday Streets, Hardly Strictly Bluegrass); most others did not state cost on the page checked, so cost is blank — not assumed ticketed.

Rebuild & verify

python3 build.py regenerates all pages from data/events.json (and rewrites data/events.csv). python3 verify.py checks: every row has an official source URL, no blank names/types/categories, SF-only exclusions are not present, internal links resolve to files, and counts match the generated pages.
