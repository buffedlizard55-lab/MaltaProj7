# San Francisco Events · source-linked snapshot

**Live site:** https://buffedlizard55-lab.github.io/MaltaProj7/

A mobile-friendly static dashboard for publicly advertised events **within the City and County of San Francisco**. The **landing page, Upcoming and Free pages contain only dated, source-confirmed free events that have not yet passed**. Admission is displayed in every row. Paid and partly free events have their own [Paid & partly free](paid.html) page; records with an unconfirmed admission cost appear on [Flagged for Review](flagged.html). The secondary recurring, special, category and all-events research catalogs retain past and other admission types, clearly labeled.

## What is actually covered

As of **October 18, 2026**: **171 published rows** (110 recurring, 61 special), **18 excluded/review records**, **58 field-level flags** and nine categories. Of the 171 published rows, **22 have both confirmed free admission and a dated upcoming/ongoing occurrence**, **63 have confirmed free admission across all dates**, **12 are partly free/conditional**, **16 have evidence of paid entry**, and **80 have no confirmed admission cost**. Registration alone or a waived fundraising minimum is **not** evidence of free or paid admission. The client recalculates upcoming status and the executive summary against the visitor’s local date; a no-JavaScript view is explicitly a snapshot as of October 18. An undated weekly/monthly tradition is **not** assigned an invented next occurrence.

Gaming and AI have no **dated** free upcoming entry verified in this snapshot, and the dashboard says so instead of promoting ticketed conferences; free programs without published future dates are catalogued with official links (The Detour Free Play Tuesday and Gaming at The Mix for gaming; AI Engineering SF for AI), with the coverage-gap flags updated on [Flagged for Review](flagged.html). Music and other free upcoming events appear with event-level publisher links. The [separate paid section](paid.html) includes paid and partly free events with their original source-stated admission wording. The [Flagged page](flagged.html) exposes unresolved source issues and every unconfirmed cost for manual review.

**Not exhaustive.** This is a reproducible, bounded collection of official-organizer, venue and government sources plus three explicitly labeled trusted civic sources. It cannot guarantee a complete list of all public, private, newly announced or unlisted events. The [method page](method.html), [source audit](research/official-source-audit.md), the [2026-10-18 catalog-wide recheck](research/verification-8c40d1c6.md) (patched by `research/patch-8c40d1c6.py`), the [2026-10-18 focus-category and upcoming-row recheck](research/verification-56415e75.md) with its [findings register](research/findings-56415e75.md) (patched by `research/patch-56415e75.py`), the [2026-10-18 final-integration recheck](research/reverification-3977d9cb.md) and its [repo review register](research/review-3977d9cb.md) (patched by `research/patch-3977d9cb.py`), the [2026-10-18 coverage-gap closure pass](research/verification-2e5715f9.md) (patched by `research/patch-2e5715f9.py`), [an earlier focused recheck](research/review-f65449c5.md), and earlier independent audits in `research/` document source decisions and unresolved issues. Always check the linked publisher for date, tickets and location before going. In particular, the YBG season end date is not an individual performance date; the organizer's homepage returned a reCAPTCHA during one pass, but the festival's own 2026 season press release is readable and confirms the free season, its May 9 opener and the October programmes.

## Rebuild and verify

No package installation is needed for the published site or its standard checks:

```sh
python3 -m unittest discover -s tests -v
python3 build.py
python3 verify.py
```

`data/events.json` is the source of truth. The build regenerates all root/category HTML pages, `assets/style.css` and `data/events.csv`. The verifier checks row sets and official source links on the free, paid and review pages; date and cost separation; duplicate IDs; local links; and data shape. It also enforces the current row and flag counts (171 published / 110 recurring / 61 special / 18 review / 58 flags), compares each cost-sorted page's rendered rows against the expected `(name, date-rank)` identity set so a duplicated or swapped row cannot hide behind a shared official URL, checks that the Gaming → Music → AI → Everything else focus sections keep that order and stay date-first inside each block, and rejects placeholder admission or time text, so a rebuild that silently drops rows fails instead of shipping. It **does not live-fetch all external URLs**; evidence from the earlier audits and the targeted current recheck is documented in `research/`. A local browser-DOM smoke test of the live client-side date logic is recorded in `research/review-f65449c5.md` and `research/verification-8c40d1c6.md`.

GitHub Pages is deployed by `.github/workflows/pages.yml` from `main`. Coordination, PRs, source review and deployment status are recorded in [COORDINATION.md](COORDINATION.md).

Local link verification also resolves same-page and cross-page HTML fragments (including percent-encoded IDs), rejects directory links without an index, and runs 11 isolated regression tests before Pages deployment. This is structural validation, not a live external-source crawl. The [latest independent maintenance audit](research/review-55c5243c.md) distinguishes targeted source rechecks from inherited evidence. A follow-up line-by-line structural re-verification and repo review on 2026-10-18 is in [research/reverification-04813d69.md](research/reverification-04813d69.md) and [research/review-04813d69.md](research/review-04813d69.md) (no data changes; 179 rows re-checked, Gaming 0 / Music 4 / AI 0 free upcoming honestly flagged).
