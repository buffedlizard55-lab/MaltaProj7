## Classification and irregularities

- **Do not infer price from sign-up language.** [AIDS Walk San Francisco’s official FAQ](https://sf.aidswalk.net/FAQ) says walkers have **no fundraising minimum**, not that registration is free. The existing admission field retains its exact wording; classifier now puts it in **Not stated / admission unconfirmed**, not the free list. Similarly “Registration required” (Bay to Breakers, AGU Annual Meeting, Microsoft Ignite) and “By invitation only” (J.P. Morgan Healthcare Conference) do **not** alone prove a ticket price; these rows now appear under **Flagged for Review → admission cost not established**, with the available wording and official link. We did not invent a dollar amount or change their original source-stated text.
- Paid section is strictly evidence-based: **13** published records explicitly state a ticket sale, paid pass, price or paid entry. **12** records have conditional free access, optional suggested contributions, or free portions alongside ticketed offerings; they are isolated in a separately labeled section. **43** source-confirmed free rows across all dates, only **8** still dated upcoming/ongoing on this audit date; **80** have no confirmed cost. Unknown is never shown as free. Exact source text is preserved in row detail or the flagged table.
- No currently dated confirmed-free gaming or AI row appears in this snapshot. [Day of the Devs SF 2026 organizer page](https://www.dayofthedevs.org/events/day-of-the-devs-san-francisco-edition-2026) documented free entry but the March edition is already over; [GDC 2027 pass pricing](https://gdconf.com/passes-pricing/) is paid. No free 2027 gaming/AI date was invented. Those focus sections display an honest empty-state notice.
- **Coverage gap:** An all-events census is not attainable from the available official pages; the audited collection is a bounded snapshot, not a comprehensive guarantee. Program directories and cadence-only entries remain in secondary research views and are not silently assigned future occurrences. The YBG season row uses an authorized civic publisher, not an accessible organizer schedule: nonblocking review flag added with the civic, government and organizer links.
