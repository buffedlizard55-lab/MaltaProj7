Method, sources, and findings

A reproducible snapshot of publicly advertised San Francisco city events.

Publish rule

  1. Each active row needs a title, source-stated date/cadence, San Francisco city location, organizer, and HTTPS source link.
  2. Accepted sources are official organizers, official venues, government sources, and clearly labeled trusted civic sources. The source type is visible in every row.
  3. Optional time and admission fields are not inferred. An em dash means the captured source did not state that optional value; “Not stated” in the Cost column means admission cost is not confirmed.
  4. Multi-city programs, cancelled events, outside-city events, and records missing essential evidence are not added to public tables. They stay on Flagged for Review with a source and explanation.

How date ordering and the executive summary work

  • Primary dashboard, Upcoming and Free pages contain only dated confirmed-free rows still current as of the audit. Secondary research views include completed and non-free rows; those tables place upcoming first and completed editions last.
  • A small script re-computes each row's status against the visitor's actual current date, so the "Upcoming" / "Completed" badge and the "in N days" label stay correct after the static build date.
  • The dashboard shows only confirmed-free dated events not yet past. Its nearest-events panel and 7/30/90-day counts use the visitor's local date; paid, mixed, unknown cost and undated rows stay out. The no-JavaScript view is explicitly dated to the audit snapshot.

How cost is labelled

Cost classes are derived only from the admission text that the linked official page stated. Nothing is inferred from an event's type. A waived fundraising minimum by itself does not establish free entry.

Free the source states no charge Free or partly free some programmes free, others ticketed Paid / ticketed the source states a charge, ticket sale or paid pass Not stated a free or paid admission price was not confirmed — never treated as free

Current split: 57 free, 16 paid / ticketed, 12 free or partly ticketed, 82 not stated. The rows without a confirmed cost are listed, with their source links, on the Flagged for Review page.

Findings, bounded by this dataset

  • The audit has 103 recurring and 64 dated-special verified rows across 9 categories; 71 of the 167 published rows are still upcoming as of 2026-10-18.
  • The recurring list breaks down as 14 weekly, 5 monthly, 10 seasonal, 74 annual. The classification is based on cadence, not whether an event is free or public.
  • The largest audited categories are Community & culture (42), Food & markets (25), Music & performing arts (24). This describes the verified source set, not all activity in San Francisco.
  • Most 2026 editions in this snapshot had already taken place by the October 18, 2026 check date, while several rows document confirmed 2027 editions. Rows record the audited edition, not a promise that the same dates repeat.
  • The review register makes scope and cancellation decisions visible instead of deleting difficult records or asserting unsupported city-only coverage.

Directories and coverage boundaries

Some San Francisco programming is published as a running calendar rather than a single event with a fixed cadence. Those are linked here as directories instead of forcing them into event rows:

  • San Francisco Public Library — Upcoming Events: branch programs and talks across the city system.
  • San Francisco City Guides: free volunteer-led walking tours (70+ tours, a program of SFPL); individual tours run on varying schedules via their tour finder.
  • SF Recreation and Park calendar: park programs and seasonal city events; individual dated listings (for example Scaregrove) become special-event rows.

Boundaries applied in this snapshot: recurring rows are created only when an organizer, venue, or government page states a fixed cadence (weekly, monthly, seasonal, or annual). Candidates seen only in secondary coverage or in roundup articles are not listed until directly verified. Well-known look-alikes outside the City and County are excluded: the Great Dickens Christmas Fair is at the Cow Palace in Daly City, and airport programs at SFO sit in San Mateo County.

Audit trail and maintenance

The complete row-level research matrix is in research/official-source-audit.md. The machine-readable source of truth is data/events.json; data/events.csv is an export. Before changing a row, re-check the official source and update the review reason when evidence is incomplete or conflicting.

This site is intentionally not an exhaustive claim: private, newly announced, or unlisted events can exist outside this dated source audit.

Scope: Publicly advertised events and recurring public programs located within the City and County of San Francisco. This is a dated, reproducible catalog; it does not claim to enumerate every private, newly announced, or unlisted event.
